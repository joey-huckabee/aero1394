# Aero1394 — Level 3 requirements

- Status: Directly verifiable decomposition of `L2.md`
- Last updated: 2026-08-28

## Purpose

Level 3 (L3) requirements define implementation-level obligations: exact wire
fields, masks, types, registry behavior, presentation contracts, edge cases,
and fixtures. Every L3 requirement names exactly one parent in
[L2.md](L2.md).

The former unlevelled requirement IDs are preserved by adding the `L3-` prefix:
for example, former `BIE-001` is now `L3-BIE-001`. No retired ID may be reused.
Live status and verification artifacts are generated in
[TRACE-MATRIX.md](TRACE-MATRIX.md).

## Conventions

Each entry uses this form:

> **L3-XXX-NNN** · Parent: L2-XXX-NNN · Applicability: Active · Verification: T
> Statement.

Applicability is distinct from implementation status:

| Applicability | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Required for the next applicable implementation stage |
| Evidence-limited | Required only for the currently observed evidence or record family until further evidence generalizes it |
| Deferred | Desired behavior whose prerequisite definition or implementation stage has not arrived |

Verification methods are Test (`T`), Analysis (`A`), Inspection (`I`),
Demonstration (`D`), and comparison with independent Evidence (`E`). A
non-test requirement reaches an implemented status only when an `Evidence`
field names the concrete artifact carrying that verification.

## L3-BIE: BIE container and record requirements

**L3-BIE-001** · Parent: L2-BIE-001 · Applicability: Evidence-limited · Verification: T, E
The decoder SHALL parse the observed record header as four explicit big-endian
`u32` words: data-item ID, recorder seconds, recorder microseconds, and raw
status/length.

**L3-BIE-002** · Parent: L2-BIE-001 · Applicability: Evidence-limited · Verification: T
The decoder SHALL derive stored-data length from
`status_and_length & 0x0000_FFFF` and use checked arithmetic for `16 + length`
in the current internal BIE definition.

**L3-BIE-003** · Parent: L2-BIE-001 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T, I
The decoder SHALL use the encoded length rather than assuming every record is
132 bytes.

**L3-BIE-004** · Parent: L2-BIE-002 · Applicability: Evidence-limited · Verification: T
A zero data-item word where the next record begins SHALL terminate the
current internal BIE file; bytes after it SHALL be reported rather than
silently ignored.

**L3-BIE-005** · Parent: L2-BIE-002 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T
Physical EOF before a complete header or declared stored-data body SHALL be
reported as truncation with needed/available sizes and absolute offset.

**L3-BIE-006** · Parent: L2-BIE-003 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T, I
The decoder SHALL preserve the complete raw status/length word even when the
low length bits are exposed separately. Upper value `0x40000000` SHALL be
exposed as an unresolved flag and SHALL not receive a semantic name until the
investigation in `ROADMAP.md` has independent supporting evidence.

**Evidence**: `docs/BIE-FORMAT.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `tests/fixtures/bie/README.md`

**L3-BIE-007** · Parent: L2-BIE-003 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T, I
Parsed records SHALL retain their absolute file offset and raw stored-data
bytes.

**L3-BIE-008** · Parent: L2-BIE-003 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T
Unknown data-item IDs and unsupported stored-data layouts SHALL remain
inspectable and SHALL not be mislabeled as corrupt solely because no typed
payload exists.

**L3-BIE-009** · Parent: L2-BIE-004 · Applicability: Evidence-limited · Verification: T, E
For data item `0x00005D04`, the 116-byte stored region SHALL map to Health
Status, Heartbeat, 92 application bytes, three STOF offsets, and VPC through
profile `aero1394-assumed-as5643b-v1`.

**L3-BIE-010** · Parent: L2-BIE-005 · Applicability: Active · Verification: I, T
Parsing, validation, policy, and recovery SHALL remain separate as required by
ADR-0007.

## L3-TIM: Time requirements

**L3-TIM-001** · Parent: L2-TIM-001 · Applicability: Evidence-limited · Verification: T, E
The outer recorder time SHALL decode as unsigned big-endian Unix seconds plus
unsigned big-endian microseconds for the current internal BIE definition.

**L3-TIM-002** · Parent: L2-TIM-001 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T, I
Raw seconds and microseconds SHALL remain accessible after conversion to a
calendar instant.

**L3-TIM-003** · Parent: L2-TIM-001 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T
The implementation SHALL reject or attach a validation finding to microsecond
values outside `0..999_999`; it SHALL not normalize malformed wire values
silently.

**L3-TIM-004** · Parent: L2-TIM-001 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T, I
Timestamp arithmetic SHALL widen raw `u32` seconds before addition or
multiplication and SHALL not impose a signed 2038 limit.

**L3-TIM-005** · Parent: L2-TIM-002 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T
Timezone selection and text format SHALL be independent presentation options;
ISO-8601 UTC SHALL be the default.

**L3-TIM-006** · Parent: L2-TIM-002 · Applicability: Deferred · Verification: T
Presentation SHALL support ISO-8601, year/day-of-year, recorder-summary
`DD:HH:MM:SS.ffffff`, and numeric Unix forms.

**L3-TIM-007** · Parent: L2-TIM-003 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T, I
Payload system ticks and BIE recorder time SHALL use distinct names and types.
Payload ticks SHALL not be converted to calendar time without a confirmed
epoch.

**L3-TIM-008** · Parent: L2-TIM-003 · Applicability: Deferred · Verification: T
A derived payload elapsed-seconds value SHALL preserve the raw `u64` ticks and
identify the selected tick rate and its evidence state.

**L3-TIM-009** · Parent: L2-TIM-004 · Applicability: Evidence-limited · Verification: T, I, E
Capture provenance for the supplied sample SHALL record an 80 Hz sample-attempt
rate, and the capture model SHALL support explicit 80 Hz and 100 Hz FireSpy
sampling configurations without treating either as a BIE wire field.

**Evidence**: `docs/BIE-EVIDENCE.md`, `tests/fixtures/bie/README.md`

**L3-TIM-010** · Parent: L2-TIM-004 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T
Timing analysis SHALL retain actual BIE timestamps and observed gaps rather
than synthesizing an ideal 12.5 ms or 10 ms sample grid. FireSpy sampling,
AS5643 frame rate, and payload production rate SHALL remain separate values.

## L3-PRO: Protocol-envelope and integrity requirements

**L3-PRO-001** · Parent: L2-PRO-001 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T, I
The BIE layer SHALL return stored bytes without requiring IEEE-1394, AS5643, or
application decoding.

**L3-PRO-002** · Parent: L2-PRO-002 · Applicability: Evidence-limited · Verification: T, E
The selected `0x00005D04` AS5643 profile SHALL decode Health Status, Heartbeat,
three STOF offsets, and VPC as raw `u32be` values while preserving the complete
BIE stored-data region.

**L3-PRO-003** · Parent: L2-PRO-002 · Applicability: Active · Verification: I
AS5643 results derived from the provisional priority/length split, STOF units,
or reconstructed ASM-header constants SHALL identify the selected profile and
carry an assumption-dependent indicator in APIs and output.

**L3-PRO-004** · Parent: L2-PRO-003 · Applicability: Deferred · Verification: T
VPC validation SHALL expose `Valid`, `Invalid`, `NotPresent`, and `NotChecked`
equivalent outcomes and retain the stored VPC and calculation inputs.

**L3-PRO-005** · Parent: L2-PRO-003 · Applicability: Evidence-limited · Verification: T, E
Golden tests for `0x00005D04` SHALL reconstruct ASM-header XOR `0x00005D60`
and verify the stored VPC using the word range defined in `docs/AS5643.md`.

**Evidence**: `docs/AS5643.md`, `docs/BIE-FORMAT.md`, `tests/bie_fixtures.rs`

## L3-PAY: Built-in payload requirements

**L3-PAY-001** · Parent: L2-PAY-001 · Applicability: Active · Verification: I, T
Application decoding SHALL be implemented in a separate `payload` layer; BIE
and protocol modules SHALL treat application bytes as opaque.

**L3-PAY-002** · Parent: L2-PAY-001 · Applicability: Active · Verification: I
Supported payload definitions SHALL be Rust source modules compiled into
Aero1394; runtime YAML profiles SHALL not be required.

**L3-PAY-003** · Parent: L2-PAY-002 · Applicability: Active · Verification: I, T
Each definition SHALL declare a stable name, definition version, exact size,
byte order, explicit field ranges, and match criteria.

**L3-PAY-004** · Parent: L2-PAY-003 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T, I
Registry selection SHALL use data-item ID and payload size at minimum and SHALL
allow data-code/configuration constraints when available. ID alone SHALL not be
treated as globally unique.

**L3-PAY-005** · Parent: L2-PAY-003 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T
Registry selection SHALL be deterministic and distinguish one match, no match,
and ambiguous matches.

**L3-PAY-006** · Parent: L2-PAY-003 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T
An unknown payload SHALL retain its data-item ID, context, size, and raw bytes.

**L3-PAY-007** · Parent: L2-PAY-002 · Applicability: Active · Verification: I, T
Payload fields SHALL be decoded with checked slice access and explicit byte
order; native layout casts, packed-struct reads, and unsafe code are prohibited.

**L3-PAY-008** · Parent: L2-PAY-002 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T
Declared fields that overlap unexpectedly or extend beyond payload size SHALL
fail definition validation; documented gaps SHALL remain visible.

**L3-PAY-009** · Parent: L2-PAY-002 · Applicability: Deferred · Verification: T
Raw physical values SHALL remain available when scaling, offsets, units,
enumeration labels, bitfields, arrays, or validity rules produce engineering
values.

**L3-PAY-010** · Parent: L2-PAY-004 · Applicability: Deferred · Verification: I, T, E
`msfcs_storesmassdata_b` support SHALL not be declared complete until the
supplied 92-byte structure is documented and golden-tested field by field.

**L3-PAY-011** · Parent: L2-PAY-004 · Applicability: Active · Verification: I
Adding a payload SHALL require a payload-document update, one Rust module, one
registry entry, sanitized fixtures, boundary/byte-order tests, and a
traceability update.

**Evidence**: `docs/PAYLOADS.md`, `docs/adr/0012-use-built-in-rust-payload-definitions.md`

**L3-PAY-012** · Parent: L2-PAY-004 · Applicability: Evidence-limited · Verification: I, E
The first payload definition SHALL record 60 Hz as a platform-specific
production rate and SHALL not present it as the BIE acquisition rate, FireSpy
sample-attempt rate, IEEE-1394 behavior, or AS5643 frame rate.

**Evidence**: `docs/PAYLOADS.md`, `docs/BIE-EVIDENCE.md`

## L3-OUT: Canonical data and output requirements

**L3-OUT-001** · Parent: L2-OUT-001 · Applicability: Deferred · Verification: I, T
One canonical typed Rust record SHALL feed CLI text, CSV, Parquet, and Python
adapters rather than using one adapter's representation as the internal model.

**L3-OUT-002** · Parent: L2-OUT-001 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T, I
Raw wire values and validation findings SHALL not be replaced by formatted or
derived values.

**L3-OUT-003** · Parent: L2-OUT-002 · Applicability: Deferred · Verification: T
CSV SHALL have an explicitly versioned, golden-tested header and stable column
order. IDs and diagnostic words may use fixed-width uppercase hexadecimal text.

**L3-OUT-004** · Parent: L2-OUT-002 · Applicability: Deferred · Verification: T, I
Parquet SHALL use non-lossy portable types, preserve on-wire `f32` as physical
float, and represent recorder time as a UTC microsecond timestamp.

**L3-OUT-005** · Parent: L2-OUT-002 · Applicability: Deferred · Verification: T
An inferred payload tick rate SHALL be stored once as output metadata with an
evidence state rather than repeated as an unexplained row value.

**L3-OUT-006** · Parent: L2-OUT-002 · Applicability: Active · Verification: I
Human-oriented formatting changes SHALL not be treated as machine-schema
compatibility.

**Evidence**: `docs/adr/0013-separate-canonical-data-from-output-presentation.md`, `docs/OUTPUTS.md`

## L3-TST: Fixture and verification requirements

**L3-TST-001** · Parent: L2-TST-001 · Applicability: Active · Verification: I
Sanitized test data SHALL include four consecutive sparse startup records, four
consecutive populated end records with their original zero word, and a
synthetic zero-word-only input.

**Evidence**: `tests/fixtures/bie/README.md`, `tests/fixtures/bie/startup-four-records.hex`, `tests/fixtures/bie/end-four-records.hex`, `tests/fixtures/bie/empty-recording.hex`

**L3-TST-002** · Parent: L2-TST-001 · Applicability: Active · Verification: I
Fixture metadata SHALL state byte count, expected ID, timestamps,
status/length, payload boundary, trailer words, and whether records are
consecutive.

**Evidence**: `tests/fixtures/bie/README.md`

**L3-TST-003** · Parent: L2-TST-001 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T
Tests SHALL verify fixture hex decoding itself before using fixture bytes as
parser evidence. Invalid digits and wrong byte counts SHALL fail.

**L3-TST-004** · Parent: L2-TST-002 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T
Parser tests SHALL cover valid termination, missing termination, truncation in
every fixed header word and in stored data, zero/oversized length, and bytes
after the terminator.

**L3-TST-005** · Parent: L2-TST-003 · Applicability: Active · Verification: T
Tests SHALL cover unknown payload fallback and ambiguous registry selection
before the registry is called stable.

**L3-TST-006** · Parent: L2-TST-001 · Applicability: Active · Verification: I
Any test derived from a real or simulated message SHALL preserve its provenance
and handling authorization without committing restricted source captures.

**Evidence**: `tests/fixtures/bie/README.md`, `docs/adr/0009-treat-documentation-and-test-evidence-as-deliverables.md`

## Supporting decisions and delivery gates

The requirement hierarchy retains the earlier baseline's decision and evidence
map. Live implementation status is now computed in `TRACE-MATRIX.md` instead of
being maintained manually here.

| Area | Decision or evidence | Baseline verification context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BIE framing | ADR-0005, ADR-0006, `BIE-FORMAT.md` | `tests/fixtures/bie/*.hex`; parser pending |
| IEEE-1394 boundary | ADR-0002, `IEEE1394.md` | Stored wire representation unidentified; controlled packet fixtures pending |
| Provisional AS5643 ASM profile | ADR-0002, `AS5643.md`, `BIE-FORMAT.md` | VPC checks in `tests/bie_fixtures.rs` |
| Error policy | ADR-0007 | Existing forensic boundary tests; BIE corrupt cases pending |
| Built-in payloads | ADR-0012, `PAYLOADS.md` | First payload preserved; complete field definition pending |
| Canonical outputs | ADR-0013, `OUTPUTS.md` | Provisional CSV row and Parquet mapping |
| CLI/Python parity | ADR-0008 | Existing CLI/library hexdump tests |
| Evidence staging | ADR-0009, ADR-0011 | Documentation and fixture set |

The next implementation slice may claim Stage 2 BIE framing only after the
active BIE and fixture requirements have automated tests. Built-in payload
support begins when the remaining payload field definition is supplied; the
provisional float interpretation alone is not an implementation contract.
