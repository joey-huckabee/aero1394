# IEEE-1394 wire-format evidence

- Status: Stored wire representation not yet identified; decoder deferred
- Last updated: 2026-08-28

## Purpose

This document records the evidence needed to identify and decode IEEE-1394 wire
representations across Aero1394 input sources. It is the future home for the
wire-level field layout, byte and bit numbering, packet families, and CRC
coverage once those details are supported by controlled evidence.

## Current evidence gap

No supplied, authorized evidence currently exposes an identifiable complete
IEEE-1394 wire packet. The available bytes do not establish whether a source
retained a physical-layer event, link packet, asynchronous stream, completed
transaction, normalized message, or another representation. Container-specific
limitations belong in each container's format definition.

## Evidence needed to define the decoder

Before an IEEE-1394 decoder contract is added, obtain:

- the definition of the IEEE-1394 representation exposed by at least one input
  adapter;
- the exact applicable IEEE-1394 revision;
- a controlled packet with known source, destination, transaction or stream
  type, payload length, and expected CRC values;
- an independent wire/analyzer view of the same event; and
- examples covering every retained event family, including any bus-reset,
  acknowledge-only, prefix-only, PHY, asynchronous, or isochronous records.

## Current conclusion

No IEEE-1394 wire-packet layout or decoder behavior is currently specified.
When controlled evidence identifies a stored representation, this document
will become the wire-level specification and will define field layout,
byte/bit numbering, CRC coverage, supported packet families, and unsupported
cases.

The container-independent AS5643 contract remains separate in
[`AS5643.md`](AS5643.md). Observations specific to how BIE stores, removes, or
normalizes downstream material belong in [`BIE-FORMAT.md`](BIE-FORMAT.md).
