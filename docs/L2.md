# Aero1394 — Level 2 requirements

- Status: Architectural decomposition of `L1.md`
- Last updated: 2026-08-28

## Purpose

Level 2 (L2) requirements derive architectural behavior from the product
outcomes in [L1.md](L1.md). Every L2 requirement names exactly one L1 parent.
Implementation-specific offsets, masks, types, edge cases, and fixture content
are specified in [L3.md](L3.md).

Live status is generated in [TRACE-MATRIX.md](TRACE-MATRIX.md); this file holds
specification content and parent relationships.

## Conventions

Identifiers use `L2-<CATEGORY>-<NNN>`. Verification methods are Test (`T`),
Analysis (`A`), Inspection (`I`), Demonstration (`D`), and comparison with
independent Evidence (`E`).

## L2-BIE: BIE container and record decoding

#### L2-BIE-001

**Parent**: L1-BIE-001

**Statement**: The BIE decoder SHALL frame records from explicit wire fields
and encoded lengths using checked arithmetic rather than a fixed observed
record size.

**Rationale**: The current sample has a repeated size, but the encoded length
is the only defensible boundary for additional record families.

**Verification Method**: T, E

#### L2-BIE-002

**Parent**: L1-BIE-001

**Statement**: The BIE decoder SHALL distinguish valid termination from
physical EOF and SHALL report incomplete headers, incomplete bodies, and
trailing bytes with actionable offsets and sizes.

**Rationale**: Silent acceptance of truncated or unexplained data would make
forensic results appear more complete than their source.

**Verification Method**: T

#### L2-BIE-003

**Parent**: L1-BIE-001

**Statement**: Parsed BIE records SHALL retain raw metadata, stored bytes, and
source location, including when no typed downstream decoder matches.

**Rationale**: Unknown data is evidence, not corruption by definition.

**Verification Method**: T, I

#### L2-BIE-004

**Parent**: L1-BIE-001

**Statement**: Support for the observed data-item family SHALL locate its
application payload without assigning names to unconfirmed envelope words.

**Rationale**: The supplied captures establish useful byte boundaries but do
not establish every field meaning.

**Verification Method**: T, E

#### L2-BIE-005

**Parent**: L1-BIE-001

**Statement**: BIE parsing, validation, caller policy, and corruption recovery
SHALL remain separate responsibilities.

**Rationale**: A forensic caller and a strict ETL caller need the same parser
facts but may apply different policies.

**Verification Method**: T, I

## L2-TIM: Recorder and payload time domains

#### L2-TIM-001

**Parent**: L1-TIM-001

**Statement**: The supported BIE recorder timestamp SHALL preserve its raw
wire fields, validate subsecond range, and convert without signed-width or
silent-normalization errors.

**Rationale**: Correct conversion must not erase malformed input or introduce
an artificial 2038 boundary.

**Verification Method**: T, E

#### L2-TIM-002

**Parent**: L1-TIM-001

**Statement**: Calendar-time format and timezone SHALL be presentation choices
independent of the canonical recorder timestamp.

**Rationale**: Output preference must not change decoded meaning.

**Verification Method**: T

#### L2-TIM-003

**Parent**: L1-TIM-001

**Statement**: Payload system ticks SHALL use a distinct type and SHALL not be
converted to calendar or elapsed time without an explicit rate and evidence
state.

**Rationale**: Payload tick rate and platform payload-production cadence are
not the FireSpy recorder cadence and do not establish an epoch.

**Verification Method**: T, I, E

## L2-PRO: Protocol-envelope and integrity handling

#### L2-PRO-001

**Parent**: L1-PRO-001

**Statement**: The BIE layer SHALL return stored bytes without requiring
IEEE-1394, AS5643, or application decoding.

**Rationale**: Each layer must be independently useful and testable.

**Verification Method**: T, I

#### L2-PRO-002

**Parent**: L1-PRO-001

**Statement**: A supported protocol-envelope decoder SHALL preserve raw words
and clearly distinguish confirmed semantics from provisional candidates.

**Rationale**: Evidence currently supports placement and correlations more
strongly than the names of every protocol field.

**Verification Method**: T, I, E

#### L2-PRO-003

**Parent**: L1-PRO-001

**Statement**: Integrity validation SHALL represent checked, unchecked,
absent, and invalid outcomes while retaining stored values and calculation
inputs.

**Rationale**: A boolean cannot distinguish missing evidence from a failed
check.

**Verification Method**: T, E

## L2-PAY: Built-in application payload decoding

#### L2-PAY-001

**Parent**: L1-PAY-001

**Statement**: Application decoding SHALL live behind a separate payload layer
whose definitions are ordinary Rust modules compiled into Aero1394.

**Rationale**: This enforces the accepted no-runtime-YAML boundary and keeps
program knowledge out of generic parsers.

**Verification Method**: T, I

#### L2-PAY-002

**Parent**: L1-PAY-001

**Statement**: Each payload definition SHALL be self-describing, range-safe,
explicit about wire representation, and lossless when producing engineering
values.

**Rationale**: Native-layout casts and implicit conversions are unsafe wire
contracts and make gaps or overlaps difficult to audit.

**Verification Method**: T, I

#### L2-PAY-003

**Parent**: L1-PAY-001

**Statement**: One deterministic registry SHALL select definitions using all
available context and SHALL distinguish one match, no match, and ambiguity.

**Rationale**: A data-item ID is not guaranteed to be a globally unique schema
identifier.

**Verification Method**: T, I

#### L2-PAY-004

**Parent**: L1-PAY-001

**Statement**: Payload support SHALL follow a repeatable documentation,
module, registry, fixture, test, and traceability workflow, with inferred
platform rates labeled at the payload boundary.

**Rationale**: Additional structures must be addable without special cases or
mixing a platform-specific 60 Hz production rate with FireSpy's 80 Hz or
100 Hz operation.

**Verification Method**: T, I, E

## L2-OUT: Canonical data and output adapters

#### L2-OUT-001

**Parent**: L1-OUT-001

**Statement**: A canonical typed Rust representation SHALL preserve raw wire
values and validation findings independently of all output adapters.

**Rationale**: The internal model must remain lossless and reusable.

**Verification Method**: T, I

#### L2-OUT-002

**Parent**: L1-OUT-001

**Statement**: Machine-output schemas and human presentation formats SHALL be
explicit, versioned where compatibility matters, and derived from the same
canonical representation.

**Rationale**: Stable CSV, Parquet, CLI, and Python behavior requires contracts
that are not accidental side effects of one adapter.

**Verification Method**: T, I

## L2-TST: Verification evidence and fixtures

#### L2-TST-001

**Parent**: L1-TST-001

**Statement**: The repository SHALL maintain a sanitized fixture corpus with
provenance, expected boundaries, and strict fixture decoding.

**Rationale**: Tests are only useful evidence when their source, scope, and
exact bytes are reviewable.

**Verification Method**: T, I, E

#### L2-TST-002

**Parent**: L1-TST-001

**Statement**: Parser verification SHALL cover valid framing and termination,
every incomplete fixed boundary, declared-body truncation, invalid lengths,
and unexplained trailing bytes.

**Rationale**: Boundary defects are the highest-risk failure mode in an
evidence-limited binary parser.

**Verification Method**: T

#### L2-TST-003

**Parent**: L1-TST-001

**Statement**: Payload-registry verification SHALL cover unknown and ambiguous
selection before the registry is considered stable.

**Rationale**: Happy-path matching alone cannot establish safe extensibility.

**Verification Method**: T
